Real dapps usually gate the signing step behind eligibility — a balance, an allowlist, a mint window — so there is often no way to reach a signature prompt on demand just to see what one looks like. This page calls eth_signTypedData_v4 directly, with a payload you control.

Nothing here moves funds. Signing typed data produces an off-chain signature; no transaction is sent and no gas is spent. But a signature IS authorisation, so only ever sign payloads you understand — that is the entire reason ClearSign exists. The examples below are safe to sign because they point at example contracts.

Works with any EVM wallet — Rabby, Frame, Coinbase Wallet, Brave, a browser's built-in wallet, MetaMask. If you have more than one installed they announce themselves and you choose, rather than whichever loaded last silently winning.

ClearSign never asks you to add a chain — that prompt is a common drainer trick, so if a site you do not trust asks you to add one, treat it as a reason to stop.

Your wallet will show its own summary — "Spending cap: Unlimited", the spender, the network. It will not show you the JSON that ClearSign reads. Use Copy this payload above, or the Check it button, which carries it over for you.

If your wallet will not sign

  • "Unknown chain" — the payload's chainId is not the chain your wallet is on. Use current chain above, or switch networks in the wallet.
  • The domain does not match — that is not an error here. A signature is valid regardless; whether a contract accepts it is a separate question, and it is exactly what ClearSign answers.
  • No prompt appears — the wallet may be locked, or the request was rejected silently. Unlock and try again.

No server, no accounts, no analytics. This page only asks your wallet to sign; it never sends a transaction and never sees your key.
